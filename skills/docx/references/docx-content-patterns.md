# DOCX content patterns

## Report

Use a title page only when useful. Follow it with an executive summary, scope and assumptions, findings organized by Heading 1 and Heading 2, evidence tables or figures, recommendations, conclusion, and references. Keep the executive summary independently understandable.

## Resume

Use name and contact line, summary, experience, education, skills, and optional projects or certifications. Lead each achievement with an action verb and measurable outcome. Prefer plain headings and avoid text boxes, columns, icons, or decorative graphics when applicant-tracking extraction matters.

## Proposal

Use problem, objectives, approach, deliverables, timeline, assumptions, risks, team, pricing or commercial terms, and next steps. Make every promised deliverable testable and distinguish commitments from optional enhancements.

## Letter

Use date, recipient, subject, salutation, short body paragraphs, closing, and signature. Keep the tone appropriate to the relationship and make the requested action explicit.

## Form or checklist

Use a clear title, instructions, grouped fields, labels, enough writing space, and a completion or approval area. Use tables only when they improve alignment and accessibility.
